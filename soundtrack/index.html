  1. 1
    The Story of Willy
    King Missile
    The epigraph. Willy’s last morning is the story’s first breath — unhurried, absurd, tender. The whole rapture is already here.
  2. 2
    Good Morning Good Morning
    The Beatles
    The waking. The record clicks on and morning floods the room — cannabis smoke, Beatles noise, the ordinary made sacred.
  3. 3
    Nuclear War
    Yo La Tengo
    The weight beneath the morning. Drone and dread sitting quietly alongside the domesticity. The apocalypse was always already here.
  4. 4
    It’s the End of the World as We Know It
    R.E.M.
    And we feel fine. The party in Central Park — everyone bopping to whatever sound is found, forty-nine minutes to go.
  5. 5
    Avant Gardener
    Courtney Barnett
    Anxious mundane. The body doing ordinary things while the mind reaches for something it can’t name. Pre-dust restlessness.
  6. 6
    Pastorale
    Kathleen Yearwood
    The hinge. Haunting pastoral breath between Barnett’s anxiety and Delt’s dissolution. A track you lean toward.

  7. 7
    Some Sunsick Day
    Morgan Delt
    Hazy dissolution. The dream being told slowly — swirling, strange, not quite frightening. Warm disorientation.
  8. 8
    Chemtrails
    Beck
    Smoke trails over the park. The MC says two minutes. The sky is full of missiles and we are still dancing.
  9. 9
    1999
    Prince
    We partied like it was. Because it was. Not a care in the world — that kind of care just stopped mattering.
  10. 10
    Amour Fou
    Maria in the Shower
    The dust settles on your face, on your hair, on mine. Strange love in the swirl — the most beautiful effect.
  11. 11
    Hide and Seek
    Imogen Heap
    Naked and alone. The fan off, the dust settling into corners. Something so vast and somehow still SO Loving.
